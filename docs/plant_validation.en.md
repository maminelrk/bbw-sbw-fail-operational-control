# Allocator-free open-loop plant validation

## Objective

Validate the signs, force balance, actuator dynamics, tire saturation, load transfer, and small-angle handling response of the vehicle plant before connecting the control allocator.

The executable MATLAB entry point is:

```matlab
plantSummary = run_plant_validation();
```

Generated evidence is written to `validation/results/plant/`.

## Scenarios and acceptance checks

| ID | Input after 0.5 s | Main checks |
|---|---|---|
| `PLANT-BRK-01` | Four equal brake forces of -1500 N | Negative longitudinal acceleration, zero yaw, straight path, forward load transfer |
| `PLANT-STR-01` | +1° road-wheel steering, no braking | Positive yaw response, bounded sideslip, friction compliance, final yaw rate within 20% of the analytical linear bicycle reference |
| `PLANT-CMB-01` | +1° steering and four brake forces of -1000 N | Positive yaw with deceleration, visible friction-ellipse coupling, positive wheel loads, bounded sideslip |

All cases also require:

- finite states and forces;
- normal load of at least 49.9 N at every wheel;
- lateral tire force no greater than the calculated combined-slip capacity;
- steering and brake-force state rates within their configured limits.

## Independent equation-level cross-check

MATLAB is not installed in the authoring environment. The same plant equations were therefore mirrored independently in Python and integrated with the same 2 ms fourth-order Runge-Kutta step. This cross-check is useful for detecting equation, sign, and threshold errors, but it is not the official MATLAB validation record.

| Scenario | Final speed | Peak absolute yaw rate | Final yaw rate | Peak absolute sideslip | Minimum wheel load | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `PLANT-BRK-01` | 3.549 m/s | 0 rad/s | 0 rad/s | 0 rad | 2359 N | Sanity checks passed |
| `PLANT-STR-01` | 16.608 m/s | 0.0749 rad/s | 0.07124 rad/s | 0.00453 rad | 2914 N | Sanity checks passed |
| `PLANT-CMB-01` | 7.885 m/s | 0.0706 rad/s | 0.04544 rad/s | 0.00567 rad | 2453 N | Sanity checks passed |

For `PLANT-STR-01`, the analytical steady-state linear bicycle reference is 0.07149 rad/s. The nonlinear plant result differs by approximately 0.35%, which supports the small-angle implementation.

No scenario exceeded its friction-ellipse lateral-force capacity in the independent cross-check.

## Interpretation

The equation-level results support the following limited claims:

- the sign conventions are coherent;
- symmetric braking does not generate yaw in a symmetric vehicle state;
- longitudinal braking transfers normal load toward the front axle;
- positive steering generates positive lateral and yaw response;
- the simplified Pacejka model agrees closely with the linear bicycle model at a one-degree input;
- combined braking reduces the lateral-force budget.

They do not validate a specific physical vehicle. Tire shape coefficients, CG height, track width, and actuator limits still contain documented assumptions. The final Gate 2 evidence must be generated by running the committed MATLAB implementation and reviewing its plots.

## Model limitations

- quasi-static load transfer without roll or pitch states;
- one axle-lumped lateral tire model at each axle;
- no wheel rotational dynamics or slip-ratio state;
- no aerodynamic drag or rolling resistance;
- one equivalent steering effector rather than two independent steering channels;
- no parameter identification against measured vehicle data.
